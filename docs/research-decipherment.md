# Research — decipherment & language mechanics (for the strata/scripts/lexicon)

> 2026-06-11 research pass (web). Question: what makes decipherment/translation mechanics
> **deep and emotionally resonant**, distilled for *Scraped Again*'s five scripts/strata,
> the G9 block-name discovery, and the seeded lexicon. Feeds [`game-depth.md`](game-depth.md)
> and the big forks in [`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md). Sibling of
> [`research-automation-depth.md`](research-automation-depth.md).

## 1. The hypothesis → evidence → validation loop (Chants of Sennaar)

**P1. Guesses are first-class, rendered everywhere.** Sennaar renders your free-text guess
above *every* occurrence of a glyph; you watch your own theory hold or fail in context. →
*A guessed meaning/name renders over every occurrence of that sign in a visibly provisional
style (flickering, lower-fi); confirmation upgrades the rendering. A purely visual
certainty gradient.*

**P2. Validate in small batches, not per-glyph.** Sennaar locks translations by matching a
page of 3–5 glyphs at once (the Obra Dinn verify-in-threes trick) — kills brute force,
makes lock-in a chord. → *Stratum "index stones" (landmarks) validate 3–4 pending
hypotheses together — the stratum's recurring epiphany beat.*

**P3. Pace certainty — never fully sure, never fully lost.** Three live populations at all
times: confirmed / guessed / unseen. The joy is the middle state. → *A found block name
stays a hypothesis until seen a second time in a different context, or used once.*

**P4. Each script gets a different *shape* of difficulty, not just different glyphs.**
Sennaar's languages differ in grammar/worldview; its weakest stretch is where that stopped.
→ *Latin/Records = the near-substitutive on-ramp; Greek/Schematics = compositional/affix
(derivable); Hiragana/Rites = formulaic/liturgical (repetition is the clue);
Runic/Relics = terse, order-free; Galactic/Signals = noisy/degraded.*

## 2. Uncertainty as texture, not failure (Heaven's Vault)

**P5. Withhold confirmation; wrongness is informative behavior, never a red X.** Ingold:
instant confirmation → brute force → nobody looks at word construction. → *The console can
run a mis-read block — it does something slightly off, or answers with a soft in-script
error. The dead system responds; it never grades.*

**P6. Compositional vocabulary makes partial knowledge generalize.** Ancient builds words
from a few dozen primitives ("water"+"person"="sailor"); late-game reading feels like
fluency, not lookup. → *Block names composed from glyph morphemes (`MOVE`, `MOVE+AGAIN`,
`MOVE+NOT`): a player who's confirmed two primitives can* predict *a block they've never
found — predicted-then-found is the single highest-value emotion available here.*

**P7. Retroactivity is the payoff.** Meanings sharpen as vocabulary grows; re-reading an
early inscription and watching it resolve is the thrill. → *Re-render every catalogued
inscription through the current dictionary; the survey log diffs itself ("this entry's
reading changed"). Enormous resonance per byte given the seeded lexicon.*

**P8. No fail state; nothing physical gates on translation.** → *Gate only depth of
understanding + automation vocabulary, never traversal.* **Anti-pattern note:** Heaven's
Vault's sailing nearly sank the loop — decipherment is a low-adrenaline reward and can't
subsidize boring transit. *Our traversal (the dead world + the renderer's identity) must
carry the mood between finds — it largely already does; keep find-density honest.*

## 3. Comprehension without translation (Tunic)

**P9. Most of the language never needs decoding.** Tunic's manual communicates through
diagrams, marginalia, wear — the untranslated majority is what makes the read minority feel
earned. → *Most inscriptions stay unread elegy (the 80%); actual decoding is reserved for
the mechanical vocabulary (block names). Already our "lore implied, never read" — hold it.*

**P10. Damage encodes history.** The manual was physically destroyed before scanning. →
*Inscription* condition *as a first-class datum: eroded glyphs, palimpsests, repairs in a
different hand. A 60%-eroded name is a partial hypothesis by construction — a difficulty
dial that also carries grief.*

**P11. The ability precedes the knowledge (the Holy Cross trick).** The input works from
minute one; the manual only tells you it exists. → *The strongest version of G9: blocks are
knowledge-gates, not flag-gates — the console* always listened*; finding a name teaches you
it exists, but composing a name you've derived (P6) works early. See the open-questions
headline fork.*

## 4. Knowledge as the only progression (Outer Wilds)

**P12. If understanding is the progression, nothing else may also be.** No upgrades;
the ship log is the inventory. → *Standing audit: any system rewarding collection volume
over comprehension dilutes the theme. Block names expand* expressiveness*, not power.
(Tension with the shard/faculty layer — flagged in open-questions.)*

**P13. The log does memory work, never deduction.** Rumor-mode edges + "there's more here"
markers keep nonlinear knowledge navigable. → *The survey log auto-links inscriptions
sharing stems across strata and marks exhausted/unexhausted sites — but never
auto-translates.*

**P14. Tragedy assembled by the player from fragments.** The Nomai's melancholy is
inferential — met only through what they left, in their own voices. → *Attributable hands:
seeded per-region author quirks (glyph formation, recurring name-glyphs) so the player
recognizes an individual dead writer without being told. Asymmetric intimacy.*

## 5. Minimal-UI lessons (Sethian, 7 Days to End with You)

**P15. Speaking the dead language back is the intimacy peak.** Sethian = conversing with an
abandoned computer in its own tongue; its failure was parser rigidity. → *Our console IS
Sethian's computer done right: output/errors/logs come back in-script, partially legible;
near-miss compositions get degraded-but-sensible behavior, never a parser shrug.*

**P16. Zero confirmation eventually deflates** (7 Days: "interesting, then unsatisfying" —
nothing pushes back). → *Confirmation must exist but be behavioral (the world is the
oracle), not a UI checkmark.*

**P17. One-to-one ciphers are the floor.** Substitution = lookup table = data entry. →
*Test per stratum: if a wiki table would trivialize it, it's under-designed — except the
on-ramp, whose job is teaching the loop.*

## 6. Why dead languages feel like grief

**P18. Decoding is the only care still available.** Every inscription is half a
conversation whose other participant is unreachable. → *The lexicon generates second-person
and imperative fragments (maintenance notes, requests, apologies) so the player keeps being
addressed by people who aren't there. Running a recovered block = finishing a dead
engineer's thought — frame execution feedback as the machine recognizing its own language
after silence.*

**P19. Elegiac ≠ vague — wordlessness needs anchors.** Fragments work when concretely
*about* something. → *Seed the lexicon with concrete referents bound to nearby world
objects (this pump, that gate, the third bell). A duty roster outlives a person more
poignantly than a lament.*

## 7. Anti-patterns checklist

| Anti-pattern | Seen in | Our guard |
|---|---|---|
| Lookup-table grinding | cipher indies, Sennaar's endgame minigame | non-bijective scripts (P17); auto-read mastered glyphs |
| Guess-the-verb rigidity | Sethian | behavioral near-miss tolerance (P5/P15) |
| Unconfirmable forever | 7 Days | world-as-oracle confirmation (P2/P16) |
| Instant confirmation | (counterfactual per Ingold) | hypothesis interval (P3) |
| Boring connective tissue | Heaven's Vault sailing | traversal carries mood / stays short |
| A second progression currency | genre default | names expand expressiveness, not power (P12) |
| Game's certainty lags the player's | Heaven's Vault late | let the player force-commit a reading ("attest") |

## Top 8 recommendations (ranked by leverage)

1. **The console always listened** (P11+P6): knowledge-gates + compositional names —
   derivation as epiphany. *The headline fork — human's call.*
2. **Hypothesis state with in-world propagation** (P1/P3): provisional renders everywhere;
   confirmed by second-sighting/first-use. Cheap; creates the whole certainty arc.
3. **Behavioral confirmation, never UI judgment** (P5/P16).
4. **Retroactive re-rendering of the survey log** (P7) — highest resonance per
   engineering-hour given the seeded lexicon exists.
5. **Five scripts = five puzzle shapes** (P4/P17); one substitutive on-ramp.
6. **Batch validation at index stones** (P2).
7. **Attributable hands + concrete referents** (P14/P19) — grief via logistics.
8. **Single-progression audit** (P12) — recurring design test.
